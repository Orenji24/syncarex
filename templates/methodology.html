Pipeline

How Synthetic Data Is Generated

The app detects CSV metadata, trains a CTGAN tabular synthesizer, generates new rows, applies row-level clinical margin controls, and builds a validation report.

1Upload

CSV data is stored with a unique run ID.

2Metadata

Column types are detected by SDV.

3Synthesis

CTGAN learns the table structure and samples new records.

4Validation

Plots, utility scores, exact-match risk, and margin checks are returned.
