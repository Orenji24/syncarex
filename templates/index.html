Examiner overview

Methodology and privacy in one view

01

Upload and schema detection

The system accepts structured healthcare CSV files and automatically detects numeric and categorical columns using SDV metadata.

02

Generative model

CTGAN learns the statistical structure of the table and samples new synthetic records for research and model prototyping.

03

Medical constraints

Age, blood pressure, and cholesterol are bounded row-by-row. Gender and medication are preserved exactly when those columns exist.

04

Privacy layer

The dashboard reports epsilon, privacy level, exact-match risk, duplicate rate, and explains that lower epsilon means stronger privacy.

05

Quality evaluation

KL divergence, KS score, correlation similarity, heatmaps, and real-vs-synthetic profile graphs show how closely patterns are preserved.

06

Research output

The app generates a downloadable synthetic CSV, dashboard metrics, medical checks, and a research report suitable for project explanation.
