What the project does

CSV Upload

Users upload structured healthcare records such as age, gender, medication, BP, glucose, cholesterol, and diagnosis fields.

Synthetic Generation

SDV CTGAN learns tabular patterns and creates new synthetic records for research and model prototyping.

Clinical Rules

Heart rate, blood pressure, and blood sugar follow source trends. Gender and medication are preserved when present.

Condition Logic

Condition is generated from clinical thresholds: BP above 120/80 gives hypertension, sugar above 140 gives diabetes.

Quality Metrics

The app reports KL divergence, KS score, duplicate rate, correlation similarity, and real-vs-synthetic profile charts.

Research Report

A built-in report page summarizes dataset structure, model method, privacy explanation, and evaluation results.

Privacy

Privacy explanation

The dashboard includes an epsilon privacy panel, duplicate-rate check, and exact-match risk score. Lower epsilon indicates stronger privacy and usually lower similarity. In this prototype, epsilon is simulated for explanation; formal Differential Privacy would require DP-SGD or another privacy-accounted method.
